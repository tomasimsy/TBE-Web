01

Engineering & Construction

Our team collaborates closely with clients, contractors, and stakeholders to achieve each project’s long-term objectives. From early-stage planning and development through engineering, program management, and construction administration services, our exclusive focus on aviation helps airports grow safely, efficiently, and strategically while maintaining continuous airport operations.

Our Comprehensive Airside & Landside Infrastructure
Services include:

  • Runways (New, Rehabilitation, and Expansion)
  • Taxiways (New, Rehabilitation, and Expansion)
  • Aircraft Aprons and Ramps (New, Rehabilitation, and Expansion)
  • Hangar and Terminal Development and Renovation
  • Runway Safety Areas
  • Airfield Lighting Systems
  • NAVAIDs
  • Storage Facilities
  • Access Roads
  • Parking Lot Improvements
  • Hangar Site Development
  • Airport Safety and Security Improvements
  • Aircraft Ramp Hydrant Fueling Systems
  • Fuel Farms (New and Expansion)

02

Planning Services

We help airports navigate future growth through practical, data-driven aviation planning focused on improving operations and supporting long-term strategic objectives.

For four decades, TBE’s staff has guided clients through the airport planning process, supporting projects that upgrade technology, improve sustainability, accommodate larger aircraft, and increase air traffic capacity.

Airport Planning Support:

Develop Airport Capital Improvement Program (ACIP) packages for our clients’ annual submittals

Prepare presentations and support materials for local, state, and federal agencies

Coordinate with elected officials and agency stakeholders to support airport project funding initiatives

Collaborate with clients’ prospective tenants, as requested

Prepare pre-applications and applications for federal and state funding assistance

03

Assistance Securing Funds

TBE provides clients with a unique advantage: deep experience in grant administration and financial management, combined with close, longstanding relationships with federal, state, and local regulatory agencies.

We support all phases of the funding process, including grant request forms, reimbursement requests, and final grant closeout documentation, helping keep our clients’ aviation projects funded and moving toward completion.

Grant Administration & Funding Support:

  • Develop and manage Capital Improvement Programs (CIP) and Transportation Improvement Programs (TIP)
  • Prepare exhibits for the Federal Aviation Administration, state Departments of Transportation (DOT), and aviation agencies
  • Coordinate required/requested client meetings with the FAA, state DOTs, and aviation agencies
  • Support development of funding request presentations for proposed airport projects
