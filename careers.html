Our Benefits

401(k)
With company match

TBE matches 50% of employee 401(k) contributions, up to 6% of salary, providing a maximum company match of 3%.

03 Years
Fully vested

Company matching contributions vest incrementally over three years, with employees becoming fully vested after three years of service.

100%
Professional development support

TBE fully reimburses employees for E.I., F.E., and P.E. exam preparation courses, study materials, and professional development resources.

25
PTO days

Full-time employees start accruing 25 days of paid time off annually, including 10 vacation days, 7 sick days, and 8 company-paid holidays.
